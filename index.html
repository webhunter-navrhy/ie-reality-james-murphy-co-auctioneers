Description

James Murphy Kinsale Property is delighted to present this development opportunity, currently laid out as a cosy 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom detached early 1900's Irish Cottage, The property stands on 1.186 Acres ( 0.48 Hectares) and has front and rear gardens with ample private car parking. It is standing on a private, spacious East to South facing site. The property lends itself to a new build or extension and renovation to the current dwelling, which it presents as an excellent opportunity for someone who wants to live in a sought-after area and would not qualify for planning permission otherwise.

This house has a floor area of circa 600 sq ft. (circa 55 Sq. m.) with accommodation consisting of an open-plan fully fitted kitchen cum dining and a sitting room with an old open fireplace (with original swing pot and kettle fireplace insert), 3 double bedrooms with mezzanine, 1 main family bathroom; The property lends itself as the perfect family home or holiday home and has scope to be enlarged to take further advantage of its setting and spacious site.

Location:

Dunderrow Village is only a 5-minute drive from Kinsale, one of Ireland's most picturesque, popular, and fashionable towns. It is situated only 17 miles from Cork City, making it ideal for commuters. The pretty seaside town of Kinsale is a mere 25-minute drive from the cosmopolitan city of Cork. This area of South West Ireland is of outstanding beauty with its dramatic coastline, unspoilt locations, and safe beaches.

Leisure activities are well catered for, Kinsale provides excellent sailing, yachting and fishing facilities from its two marinas. There are three world-class golf courses, including the renowned Old Head of Kinsale. Other recreational pursuits include walking, bathing, surfing, and horse riding. There are numerous cultural events held throughout the year, such as the world-famous Kinsale Arts Festival, the Cork Jazz Festival, the Cork Film Festival, and the Kinsale Gourmet Festival, along with a thriving music and opera programme.

Cork has several fine theatres, and the Cork Opera House hosts many exceptional productions yearly. Kinsale is also home to some of Ireland's finest restaurants, contributing to its title as Gourmet Capital of Ireland. It also has many top-class hotels and lots of welcoming pubs. Cork International Airport is conveniently located 20 minutes' drive from Kinsale, with direct links within Ireland and to Major European and UK cities.
